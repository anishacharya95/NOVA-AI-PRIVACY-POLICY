Our approach

Nova AI is designed to keep chat history and provider credentials on your device. To generate a response, the content you submit is sent to the AI service you choose. Some account and conversation-list metadata may be processed by Firebase when account features are configured.

01 At a glance

  • Chat messages are stored locally on your device; Firestore stores conversation-index metadata, not message bodies.
  • Your prompt and relevant conversation context go to the AI provider and model you select.
  • Nova Free routes requests through a Cloudflare Worker to an upstream model provider.
  • Firebase Authentication and Firestore handle account and limited conversation-index data when configured.
  • Provider API keys are encrypted with Android Keystore-backed AES-GCM and are not uploaded or synced by the app.
  • Nova AI integrates Google Mobile Ads and UMP consent SDKs for advertising.

02 Data stored on your device

Nova AI stores chat messages and conversation details, attachment references (such as content URI, display name, MIME type and size), provider settings, encrypted API keys, assistants, system prompts, app preferences, and AI response reports in private app storage.

Image bytes are read when you send an image with a message. The app documentation says non-image attachment contents are not uploaded; filenames and MIME types may be included as text references.

Documented Android backup rules exclude these data stores from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Chat content is protected by Android's app sandbox but is not described as separately encrypted at rest. Provider API keys receive additional Keystore-backed encryption.

Account information

When Firebase account functionality is configured, Firebase Authentication processes your email address and authentication data. The Firestore profile may contain your Firebase user ID, email address, optional display name, and account timestamps. The conversation index contains titles (up to 160 characters), timestamps, message counts, and sync timestamps—not message bodies, attachments, API keys, or safety reports.

03 Information shared with AI providers

When you submit a chat request, Nova AI sends the selected provider your prompt, the conversation history included in the request, your system prompt or assistant instructions, and your provider API key when required.

Images are sent with the request only when the selected model supports vision. Non-image attachment filenames and MIME types may be sent as text references; file contents are not uploaded according to the app documentation.

Documented provider presets include OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Puter, NVIDIA NIM, Cohere, Groq, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, Together AI, SiliconFlow, Nova Free, custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints, and local services such as Ollama and LM Studio. Each provider has its own privacy policy, retention practices, and terms. Nova AI cannot control how independent providers retain or use submitted data. Avoid sending passwords, payment details, or confidential information unless you have assessed the provider's practices.

04 Nova Free

When you choose Nova Free, your Firebase ID token is sent to the Nova Free Cloudflare Worker for authentication. Your prompt and included conversation history are forwarded to the configured upstream model provider, and the response is streamed back.

The Worker is documented as not persisting prompts or replies. Diagnostic logs are described as containing model identifiers and HTTP status codes, not prompt text, credentials, or user identifiers. Abuse-protection counters use Firebase user ID and IP address and expire after approximately one hour. Images are not accepted on this route.

Cloudflare processes network metadata, including IP address, to host and protect the service. Upstream model providers process requests under their own terms. The upstream route depends on the model selected.

05 Image Studio

Nova Free image generation sends your prompt to the public Pollinations image endpoint. Gemini image generation sends your prompt and any selected source image to Google's Gemini API using your own API key. Generated images are held in memory for display and are written to your device gallery only when you choose Save image. The relevant external provider's privacy policy applies to its processing.

06 Permissions & device access

The reviewed Android manifest requests internet access and network-state access. It also declares legacy write-storage permission only for older Android versions where it is needed to save a generated image.

The reviewed app documentation states that Nova AI does not access your location, contacts, microphone, camera, SMS, call logs, calendar, or installed-app list. It does not read files you have not explicitly selected through the Android file picker.

07 Advertising & privacy choices

Nova AI integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display App Open ads when the app is opened or returned to the foreground, a banner in the chat screen, and an interstitial at the Image Studio exit transition. Google and its advertising partners supply these ads.

The Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), app and ad interactions (such as app launches, ad views and taps), diagnostic and performance information (such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use), and device/account identifiers (which may include the Android advertising ID and app-set identifiers where available). Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Actual processing can vary by region, device settings, consent choices and enabled ad features.

Nova AI uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to refresh consent information at app launch and show a consent form when required. Ads are requested only when UMP reports that requests are allowed. Ad requests are configured for the general-audience content rating. Google and its partners process advertising information under their own privacy terms. Nova AI does not sell personal data.

Read Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure and Privacy Policy for details.

08 Security

  • Remote provider and Firebase traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Provider API keys are encrypted using AES-GCM with a non-exportable Android Keystore key and excluded from Android backup.
  • The app does not intentionally log provider API keys or prompt text.
  • The Nova Free Worker is documented as validating Firebase tokens, limiting request sizes and concurrency, and avoiding prompt or credential logging.
  • Firestore rules are designed to restrict each user's documents to that authenticated user.

No system is perfectly secure. Firestore rules must be deployed and correctly configured in the production Firebase project for server-side restrictions to apply. If a key may have been exposed, revoke it with the provider and replace it in Nova AI settings.

09 Retention & deletion

  • Chats and attachment references: Until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall. Use message/conversation delete controls or Settings → History and data → Clear all chat history where available.
  • Provider keys and settings: Until removed in Settings → Providers or app storage is cleared.
  • Safety reports: Until deleted in Settings → Safety reports → Clear all reports where available.
  • Firebase account and cloud metadata: Until account deletion is requested through Settings → General → Delete account where available.
  • Nova Free abuse counters: Automatically expire after approximately one hour.

Account deletion is intended to remove the Firebase Authentication account, Firestore profile, conversation-index entries, and that account's local chats, providers, and assistants on the device. Firebase may require a recent sign-in. Uninstalling the app removes local data but does not automatically delete your Firebase account.

10 International & third-party processing

AI providers, Google Firebase, Cloudflare, and image-generation providers may process information on infrastructure outside your country. Their own terms and privacy policies govern their processing. Nova AI does not control the location, retention, or onward use of data by independent providers.

11 Children's privacy

Nova AI is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by applicable law in the user's country, whichever is higher. It connects to third-party generative AI services that may produce content unsuitable for minors and cannot independently verify a user's age. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with Nova AI, contact the developer.

12 AI-generated content

AI responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or inappropriate. They are not professional medical, legal, financial, or safety advice. Verify important information independently. You can report an inappropriate response using the message action menu where available.

13 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality, providers, or data practices change. Material changes will be reflected here with a revised effective date. Review this page periodically and before using new features.
